# Smart Clinic Management System

## Project Overview

The Smart Clinic Management System is a Java-based object-oriented application designed to manage the basic operations of a clinic.

The system represents important clinic entities such as users, patients, doctors, appointments, medical records, prescriptions, billing, payments, departments, rooms, administrators, and clinics.

The project demonstrates the practical application of Object-Oriented Programming concepts in Java.

## Objectives

- Manage clinic users and administrators.
- Maintain patient and doctor information.
- Manage appointments between patients and doctors.
- Maintain medical records and prescriptions.
- Handle billing and payment information.
- Manage clinic departments and rooms.
- Demonstrate relationships between different classes.
- Apply Java Object-Oriented Programming principles.

## System Modules

### 1. User Management
Manages common user information and operations.

### 2. Patient Management
Stores and manages patient-related information.

### 3. Doctor Management
Stores doctor details and provides doctor-related operations.

### 4. Appointment Management
Represents appointments between patients and doctors.

### 5. Medical Record Management
Maintains patient medical records and related information.

### 6. Prescription Management
Stores prescription information associated with patient treatment.

### 7. Billing Management
Handles billing information for clinic services.

### 8. Payment Management
Processes payment-related operations.

### 9. Clinic Management
Represents clinic information and clinic-related operations.

### 10. Department Management
Manages departments available in the clinic.

### 11. Room Management
Handles clinic room allocation and release operations.

### 12. Administration
Provides administrative functionality for managing the clinic system.
